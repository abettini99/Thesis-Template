\section{Landscape environment}

The landscape environment basically rotates your text 90 degrees counterclockwise, while also rotating the Overleaf environment so you can actually see what you are writing. You can insert a sequence of really long equations in here:

\begin{alignat*}{3}
    a &\,\,=\,\,&& \pderiv[]{a}{x} \pderiv[]{x}{y} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} + \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \pderiv[]{y}{z} \\[3mm]
    b &\,\,=\,\,&& a \exp{\big\{a + b + c + d + e + f + g + h + i\big\}}
\end{alignat*}

Alternatively, you can also insert a table that may be really wide like this:

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{r|X}
    \bfseries first column & \bfseries a really really really really really really really really really long column \\ \hline
    1            & 2    \\ \hline
    1            & 2    \\ \hline
    1            & 2    \\ \hline
    1            & 2    \\ \hline
    1            & 2 
\end{tabularx}

\clearpage

You can also see here that this will rotate the text of the opposing page (recto if previous page was verso, and verso if previous page was recto) in the same orientation so you only need to rotate the book once to read the text/graphs/tables/equations: you do not need to flip the book/report.
